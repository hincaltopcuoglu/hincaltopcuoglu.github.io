We help teams turn noisy GA4 events into reliable insights and fast CRO wins. In a recent diagnostic on a project, we analyzed raw event data and discovered critical issues: approximately **25% exact duplicates**, several same-timestamp bursts, and instrumentation loops. After deduplication and noise filtering, we realized that measuring uplift properly would be impossible without first fixing these tracking leaks.

#### What we did:
*   **Rapid Triage:** Identified duplicates, same-TS bursts, and repeating instrumentation patterns.
*   **Sessionization:** Rebuilt sessions (30-min inactivity) to compare raw vs. cleaned funnels.
*   **Leakage Detection:** Ran predictive tests and flagged suspiciously high scores (AUC > 0.99) driven by cumulative counters rather than actual behavior.
*   **Practical Output:** A short list of low-effort A/B ideas and a session-level diagnostics report.

#### Why this matters:
Good CRO requires clean signals. Running experiments on instrumented noise leads to wasted budgets and incorrect business decisions. Our approach focuses on fixing the "measurement layer" first (event debouncing, unique IDs) so that the A/B test results actually mean something.

#### Example quick wins suggested:
1.  **Fix Event Debouncing:** Ensure a single user action (like a button click) emits exactly one event.
2.  **Focus on Low-Noise Metrics:** Prioritize tests on pages where tracking is already stable (e.g., specific Landing Pages).
3.  **14-Day Sprint:** Launch a targeted campaign, measure with a clean `form_submit` event, and iterate based on data you can trust.
